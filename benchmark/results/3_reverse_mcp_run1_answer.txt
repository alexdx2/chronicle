Here is a precise, verified breakdown based on the actual source files:

---

## Producers

**`BattleResultProducer`** — `arena-api/src/arena/battle-result.producer.ts`
- Publishes to the `'battle-results'` topic (constant defined locally as `const TOPIC = 'battle-results'` and also exported from `shared/src/constants.ts` as `BATTLE_TOPIC`).
- Called by `ArenaService.tomAttacksJerry()` after every attack resolves and the `BattleEvent` is persisted to the database.

---

## Consumers

**`BattleResultConsumer`** — `spectators-api/src/spectators/battle-result.consumer.ts`
- Listens via `@EventPattern('battle-results')` (NestJS microservices Kafka decorator).
- Handler method: `handleBattleResult(event)`.
- Immediately delegates to `SpectatorService.recordBattle(event)`.

This is the **only consumer** found in the codebase. No other service subscribes to this topic.

---

## Downstream Flow

```
ArenaService.tomAttacksJerry()
  ├─ Fetches Tom & Jerry status (HTTP → tom-api, jerry-api)
  ├─ Resolves outcome: TOM_HITS | JERRY_DODGES | TRAP_TRIGGERED
  ├─ Persists BattleEvent → Prisma (arena-api DB)
  └─ BattleResultProducer.publish(event)
        └─► Kafka topic: battle-results
              └─► BattleResultConsumer.handleBattleResult(event) [spectators-api]
                    └─► SpectatorService.recordBattle(event)
                          └─ Appends event to in-memory battles[]
                                ├─ Powers GET /stats/leaderboard (StatsController)
                                │     → counts TOM_HITS vs JERRY_DODGES/TRAP_TRIGGERED wins
                                └─ Powers GET /stats/recent (StatsController)
                                      → returns last 10 battle events
```

**Note:** `NotificationService` exists in `spectators-api` and can push to an external `http://notifications:3005` service, but it is **not wired to `BattleResultConsumer`** in the code found — it is a standalone injectable not called from `recordBattle`.

`CleanupTask` in `spectators-api` runs hourly/nightly cron jobs against `SpectatorService` (leaderboard reporting, record cleanup), but these are time-driven, not event-driven from the topic.

---

## Services Involved

| Service | Role |
|---|---|
| **`arena-api`** | **Producer** — orchestrates battles, persists events, publishes to `battle-results` |
| **`spectators-api`** | **Consumer** — receives events, maintains in-memory battle history, exposes leaderboard & recent battles via HTTP |
| **`tom-api`** | Indirect upstream — queried by `arena-api` for Tom's status before publishing |
| **`jerry-api`** | Indirect upstream — queried by `arena-api` for Jerry's status and active traps before publishing |

`tom-api` and `jerry-api` have no direct relationship with the `battle-results` topic — they are only involved in the HTTP calls that precede the publish.
