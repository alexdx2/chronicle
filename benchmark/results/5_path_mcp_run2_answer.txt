## Indirect Connections (via other services)

**Via arena-api (Kafka + HTTP hub):**

arena-api sits between both services in two ways:

```
spectators-api
  └── BattleResultConsumer  ──[CONSUMES]──► battle-results (Kafka topic)
                                                    ▲
                                            [PUBLISHES]
                                         BattleResultProducer
                                              (arena-api)
                                         ArenaService
                                              │
                                         TomClient ──[CALLS_SERVICE]──► tom-api
```

- `BattleResultProducer` (arena-api) **publishes** to the `battle-results` Kafka topic (edge 55, active)
- `BattleResultConsumer` (spectators-api) **consumes** from the `battle-results` Kafka topic (edge 56, active)
- `TomClient` (arena-api) **calls** tom-api directly via `CALLS_SERVICE` (edge 89, active) and `GET /tom/status` endpoint (edge 61, active)

So arena-api is a shared hub: it produces the Kafka events that spectators-api watches, **and** it directly calls tom-api for live data during battles.
