Here's the complete picture of the `battle-results` Kafka topic:

---

## Producers

**`BattleResultProducer`** — `arena-api`
- File: `arena-api/src/arena/battle-result.producer.ts`
- Triggered by `ArenaService.tomAttacksJerry()` after a `BattleEvent` is persisted to the database via Prisma.
- The event payload includes: `tomId`, `jerryId`, `weapon`, `trap`, `result` (e.g. `TOM_HITS`, `JERRY_DODGES`, `TRAP_TRIGGERED`), and `damage`.

---

## Consumers

**`BattleResultConsumer`** — `spectators-api`
- File: `spectators-api/src/spectators/battle-result.consumer.ts`
- Listens via `@EventPattern('battle-results')` (NestJS microservices decorator).
- On receipt, calls `SpectatorService.recordBattle(event)`, which appends the event to an in-memory `battles[]` array.

---

## Downstream Flow

1. **`ArenaService.tomAttacksJerry()`** resolves the battle outcome and saves a `BattleEvent` row via Prisma.
2. **`BattleResultProducer.publish(event)`** is called, emitting the persisted event to the `battle-results` Kafka topic.
3. **`BattleResultConsumer.handleBattleResult(event)`** in `spectators-api` receives the message.
4. **`SpectatorService.recordBattle(event)`** stores it in an in-memory list.
5. That list powers two read endpoints:
   - **`getLeaderboard()`** — tallies Tom wins (`TOM_HITS`) vs Jerry wins (`JERRY_DODGES` + `TRAP_TRIGGERED`) plus total count.
   - **`getRecentBattles()`** — returns the last 10 battle events.

There is no further fan-out — `spectators-api` is the sole consumer and does not re-publish downstream.

---

## Services Involved

| Service | Role |
|---|---|
| **`arena-api`** | Producer — orchestrates battles, writes to DB, publishes to `battle-results` |
| **`spectators-api`** | Consumer — receives battle results, maintains leaderboard and recent-battle history |
| **`tom-api`** | Indirectly involved — queried by `SpectatorService` for Tom's display name (HTTP, not Kafka) |
| **`jerry-api`** | Indirectly involved — queried by `SpectatorService` for Jerry's display name (HTTP, not Kafka) |
